## 📋 Launcher Changelog v0.6.29

### ✨ New Features

#### Clip Editor
- **Its Own Window** - The editor opens in a separate, resizable window. Opening another clip asks before unsaved edits are dropped and waits while a save is still running
- **Premiere-Like Layout** - Tools, preview, inspector and timeline sit in panels you can drag to any size, with a menu bar, Ctrl+S to save, a help dialog and a button that restores the layout
- **Cut Out The Middle** - Split at the playhead and cut out any stretch. The timeline closes the gap and playback runs across the cut without a hitch
- **Picture And Sound Apart** - One switch separates the picture from the sound. Every audio track then gets its own trim handles, can be moved in time against the picture and can be cut on its own
- **Black Out Or Mute A Stretch** - Hide the picture or silence one sound track over a stretch without touching the rest
- **Overlays** - Draw a blur, a filled box, an arrow pointing in any of eight directions or text placed anywhere in its box, each in any colour from the launcher's colour picker. The preview matches the saved clip at every resolution
- **Undo And Redo** - Every edit can be undone with Ctrl+Z and redone with Ctrl+Y
- **One Save Button** - Saving only re-encodes when something has to be painted, so a plain trim is instant
- **Any Shape** - Export vertical, square, wide or untouched. The file is named after the shape it was cut to
- **GIF Export** - Turn any clip into a GIF from the gallery

#### Clip Recording
- **Record A Whole Screen** - Screens are offered next to open programs, with a warning that everything on that screen ends up in the clip
- **Keep A Program Out Of The Sound** - Pick a program, such as your music player, whose sound is left out of your clips
- **Microphone Noise Suppression** - A switch next to the microphone volume cleans background noise out of your voice
- **See How Recording Runs** - The clip settings show the graphics card, driver and encoder really in use, warn when recording runs degraded and can upload the recording log
- **Old NVIDIA Driver Warning** - The clip settings say when your driver is too old to record on the graphics card

#### Gallery
- **Clips By Month** - Clips are grouped by month, with a menu and a right click on every clip to edit, rename, cut vertical, make a GIF, show in folder or delete

#### Twitch
- **Link Twitch** - Link your Twitch account in the Socials window with a short code, choose which permissions NoRisk gets and unlink it again at any time. Unlinking also revokes the access at Twitch
- **Link From The Game** - The game can ask the launcher to link, change or unlink Twitch. The launcher comes to the front and the game picks up the new link without a restart

#### Sync Packs
- **Pack Mods In The Mods Tab** - A profile's sync pack mods are listed grouped by pack, with a "From sync pack" filter
- **Per-Profile Exclusions** - Turn a single pack mod off for just this profile, pin it to a version, turn it off in the whole pack or remove it, also for several mods at once
- **Crash Fixes For Pack Mods** - The crash check sees sync pack mods, and its suggested fixes work on them too

#### Legal
- **Updated Terms** - When our terms change you see what changed and accept once. If you already accepted the old terms you are not asked again
- **Privacy Policy Banner** - A dismissible banner tells you when the privacy policy has changed, and "learn more" opens it in your language

#### Notifications
- **Anonymous Gifts** - A gift can come from an anonymous sender and shows the message attached to it

#### CLI
- **JVM Arguments** - `launch` and `temp` take `--jvm-args="..."` for extra JVM arguments that apply to that one launch only

### 🐛 Bug Fixes

#### Clips
- **Minecraft Crash On Close** - On NVIDIA cards Minecraft could crash in `nvapi64.dll` when it was closed or resized while clips were on. The graphics driver unloaded a library the capture hook still relied on, which is now kept loaded
- **Right Graphics Card** - Recording runs on the card that holds the game's picture, tries every encoder in turn, falls back to the processor when no card can encode and rebuilds itself after a driver reset
- **Fewer Retries, Clear Reasons** - After repeated failures recording waits longer instead of retrying and toasting every few seconds, and says why it failed and when it tries again
- **No Half-Written Clips** - Clips are written to a temporary file and only renamed when finished, and free disk space is checked before a clip, trim or render is saved
- **Minimised Game** - A minimised game records black instead of freezing on its last frame
- **Scaled Displays** - Windows on scaled displays are neither cropped nor recorded smaller
- **Upside-Down Or Wrong Colours** - Hooked OpenGL frames are always turned upright, typeless and sRGB textures are read correctly and players show the clip's colours as they were recorded
- **OBS Running Too** - A capture hook that OBS already put into the game is shared instead of fought over
- **Wrong Programs** - The capture hook is never loaded into a 32-bit program or on an ARM computer
- **Stereo Exports** - Left and right stay in place when an export remixes the sound
- **Picture And Sound In Step** - Renders keep each frame's real time, encode on the graphics card first and fall back to the processor when the card fails
- **No Endless Waiting** - The editor and gallery stop waiting when the capture engine stops or refuses a render
- **Version Mismatch** - The launcher says clearly when it and the capture engine speak different protocol versions
- **Storage Limit** - The clip storage limit only ever deletes clips the launcher recorded itself, never other videos in the folder
- **Overlay Focus** - The in-game overlay no longer steals focus or stays on top of other windows
- **macOS** - No more false version error, track volumes are applied, the capture helper no longer shows in the app switcher and the editor only offers what the Mac engine can do. Overlays, cuts and separate tracks follow in a later update

#### Profiles
- **Mod Names** - Mods, dependencies and recognised files show their project name instead of the file name, and pack mods that showed a version are named correctly again

### 🧹 Chores
- **FFmpeg For The Game** - The FFmpeg libraries the game needs are installed in the background, pinned by checksum and refreshed on upgrade. Versioned Linux libraries are unpacked correctly
- **Dev Packs** - Development packs only show for staff and testers who hold the matching permission, and update live when permissions change
- **Screen Time** - With analytics consent, the launcher reports anonymously how long each tab is open
- **Capture Engine Cleanup** - The capture engine is split into smaller modules, logs every graphics card, driver, Windows build and monitor scaling at start and writes where it crashed into `capture.log`
- **Clip Editor Cleanup** - The editor is split into hooks and smaller files
- **Icon Credits** - The new icons are listed in the bundled icon credits
