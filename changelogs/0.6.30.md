## 📋 Launcher Changelog v0.6.30

### ✨ New Features

#### Clip Editor
- **Timeline Zoom** - Zoom the timeline with Ctrl + mouse wheel, Ctrl +/-/0 or the View menu. While zoomed, the mouse wheel scrolls sideways and a slim bar under the tracks shows where you are
- **Sound Of Every Clip** - Exported clips and videos from elsewhere show their sound track with a waveform in the editor

#### Clips On macOS
- **The Full Editor** - Cut out the middle, separate picture and sound, export vertical, square or wide, and draw blur, boxes, arrows and text, just like on Windows
- **GIF Export** - Turn any clip into a GIF from the gallery
- **Record A Whole Screen** - Screens are offered next to open programs
- **Keep A Program Out Of The Sound** - Leave a program such as your music player out of your clips
- **Microphone Noise Suppression** - Cleans background noise out of your voice
- **Reset Permissions** - A button resets macOS screen and input permissions that got stuck after an update

#### Launcher
- **Keep Running In The Background** - Optional: closing the window keeps the launcher running as an icon next to the clock, so a game you start keeps loading. Off by default

### 🐛 Bug Fixes

#### Profiles
- **Deleting Several Screenshots** - Deleting more than one selected screenshot at once works again
- **Folder Links Back Into The Profile** - A folder link that points back into its own profile no longer leaves the export stuck on "Loading file structure" or makes duplicating copy the profile into itself again and again. Folders linked in from a sync pack keep all their files

#### Clips
- **Clear Microphone** - The microphone no longer sounds robotic in the mixed sound track
- **Unapplied Cuts** - Saving keeps a cut you marked but did not apply yet instead of saving an unchanged copy
- **Separate Tracks** - Tracks with their own range save that range, and a picture that starts later is rendered exactly instead of jumping to the nearest keyframe
- **Exact Clip End** - Renders keep the last frame and add no extra one
- **Settings Apply Right Away** - Clip length and bitrate changes take effect without restarting the recording
- **Programs Started Later** - A program you keep out of the sound is also left out when it starts after the recording
- **Broken Saves Reported** - A saved clip that cannot be played is reported as an error, and a failed in-game save no longer stops a render in the editor
- **Clip Open In The Editor** - It can no longer be renamed or deleted, and opening the editor closes the player
- **Storage Limit** - The limit never deletes the clip that was just saved or the one open in the editor, and GIFs no longer cost you clips
- **Export Names** - A failed export frees its file name, so the next one is not called "_edited2"
- **Clips Turned Off** - Editing or exporting with clips turned off says so in your language
- **macOS** - Sound no longer goes missing or stops after 1.2 seconds, the clip system starts with the launcher, the editor and log windows open on the first click, and the colour swatch in the editor is square again

#### Capes
- **Faster Cape Browsing** - Scrolling deep into the cape list no longer slows down page after page

#### Logs
- **Log Search** - Typing in the log search no longer drops out of the field after the first letter

#### Launcher
- **Second Start** - Starting the launcher again brings the open one to the front more reliably, and on macOS clicking the Dock icon brings back a hidden window
- **Quit From The Tray** - Quitting from the tray icon asks about unsaved clip edits first, just like the close button

### 🧹 Chores
- **Linux Build** - The AppImage is packed with permissions every user can read and run
- **Tests On Linux** - The Rust tests pass on Linux again
